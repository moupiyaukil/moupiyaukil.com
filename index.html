My design & leadership philosophy

The principles I design by, and the ones I lead by.

Design philosophy

Don't make me think

Design should be simple and intuitive to use, like a smooth ride on a clear road.

Remember the users

Users belong in the design process. They will use the product, not you or me. Sharing early prototypes, gathering feedback and putting it on the roadmap brings them closer.

Measure the impact

"If you can't measure it, you can't manage it." Peter Drucker

Data-informed design aligns design and business by showing design's impact. It reduces opinion-based decisions, shows where more qualitative research is needed, and makes room for experimentation and user advocacy.

Leadership philosophy

Servant leadership

I'm here to serve my team, and to carve a path for each person's success and growth, which drives the company's success too. I also serve my "A team" of peer leaders and my leadership team, to keep us aligned on the product.

Situational leadership

Knowing my team and peers well tells me when, why and how to adapt. It lets me coach from each person's strengths.

Non-negotiables

  • Trust and radical kindness, for immediate peers and beyond.
  • Respect for the team and for oneself.
  • User-centricity, and a shameless commitment to it.
